## Device Recognition

| # | Question | Why This Matters (SQE Rationale) |
|---|----------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | How is a "known device" defined technically? Is it based on a device fingerprint, browser cookie, app-level device ID, or a hardware identifier? | Cannot set up reliable test conditions for "new" vs "known" device without knowing the mechanism. (GAP-001) |
| 2 | How long does a device remain "known"? Is there a time-to-live (TTL) after which a previously recognized device becomes "new" again? | Affects whether long-inactive users will be OTP-challenged on return. (GAP-011) |

## Location Detection

| # | Question | Why This Matters |
